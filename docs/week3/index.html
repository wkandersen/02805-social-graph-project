    03 / stress test

    Now pull people out.

    We ranked the untouched network once, removed characters in that order, and tracked the largest surviving component. This tests whether a centrality ranking actually identifies structural weak points—not merely prestigious pages.

    04

    case finding

    One character brokers far more than their degree predicts.

    Degree asks who knows many people. Betweenness asks who controls routes. The degree-preserving null is what turns that distinction from a slogan into evidence.
    Representation
    A node is a Marvel superhero Wikipedia article. An undirected, unweighted edge exists when either article links to the other. Analysis uses the 277-node giant component.
    Null model
    80 sequential degree-preserving double-edge shuffles, three swaps per edge between samples. The script asserts that every node keeps exactly the same degree.
    Important limit
    Wikipedia links measure editorial attention, not friendship or story-canon relationships. A high z-score identifies unusual linking structure, not a causal social role.
    Human check before publishing
    Pick one reported subject, count its links in the frozen edge list, and inspect two neighbours on Wikipedia. Record the checker and whether the “broker” interpretation survives.
